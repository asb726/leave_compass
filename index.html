How the forecast works

1. Start with the actual balances from your most recent pay statement and the date those balances were accurate.

2. Add your biweekly leave accruals through the end of the leave year.

3. Deduct annual leave taken since the balance date and future planned leave.

4. Any projected annual leave above your chosen carryover limit is marked additional leave to use.

Your snapshot is the starting point for projections.

Pay periods: This tracker assumes accruals are credited at the end of 14-day periods anchored to the leave-year end date.

Six-hour accrual: Credits 10 hours (rather than 6) in the last pay period of the leave year.

Special rules: Leave transfers, restored leave, changes in your accrual rate, uncommon tours, or a personal leave ceiling may require a custom setting or balance adjustment.
