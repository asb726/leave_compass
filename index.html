How the forecast works

1. Start with the actual balances from your most recent pay statement and the date those balances were accurate.

2. Add your biweekly leave accruals through the end of the leave year.

3. Deduct annual leave taken since the balance date and future planned leave.

4. Any projected annual leave above your chosen carryover limit is marked additional leave to use.

How recommendations are built

1. Start with your current projected use-or-lose after already scheduled annual leave.

2. Find work shifts still available before the leave-year ends, using your recurring cycle and date overrides.

3. Prefer shifts beside known RDOs and existing leave, creating longer continuous time away from work.

4. Select full remaining shifts until the annual leave target is covered. Review dates and request leave through the FAA's normal process.

Holiday treatment is never assumed from the standard federal calendar. Enter your confirmed in-lieu holiday hours for any applicable shift. Overtime entries are excluded from annual-leave chargeable hours and stop an OT day from being treated as a full day off for break optimization. Staffing, leave bidding and approval are not evaluated.

Pay periods: This tracker assumes accruals are credited at the end of 14-day periods anchored to the leave-year end date.
